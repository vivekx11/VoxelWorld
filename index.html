How To Play

Open World Lobby

Spawn in a 25×25 world with grass terrain, a dirt mountain in the corner, and a stone castle to explore. Place blocks, break terrain, or just vibe.

  • ✓ 30×30 terrain with grass layer
  • ✓ Stone castle with 4 walls
  • ✓ Dirt mountain structure
  • ✓ Full block placing & breaking
  • ✓ Press Ctrl+S to enter Level 1
🏆

Parkour Challenge

20 platforms stretch out before you. Wrong platforms collapse the moment you step on them. Only the correct path leads to the gold finish block.

  • ✓ 20 platform stages
  • ✓ Wrong platforms crumble & fade
  • ✓ Fall → respawn at start
  • ✓ Reach gold block to win
  • ✓ Press ESC to return to lobby

Creative Building

Left-click to place blocks on any surface, right-click to break. Use the hotbar or 1–7 keys to select your material. Build anything you can imagine.

  • ✓ 7 material types
  • ✓ Left click = Place block
  • ✓ Right click = Break block
  • ✓ 1–7 hotbar quick-select
  • ✓ Base layer protected from deletion
